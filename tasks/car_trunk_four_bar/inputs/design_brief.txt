CONCEPTUAL CAR TRUNK FOUR-BAR HINGE

Coordinate system: meters, +Z up, mechanism motion in the X-Z plane, thickness along Y.

Fixed pivots:
A = (-0.320, 0.000, 0.280) m
B = ( 0.280, 0.000, 0.200) m

Center-to-center link lengths:
ground A-B = 0.60531 m
input A-C = 0.44000 m
lid coupler C-D = 0.58000 m
follower B-D = 0.56000 m

Use the upper circle-intersection assembly branch. Drive input angle A-C from 80 degrees
at frame 1 (closed) to -10 degrees at frame 80 (open), solving C and D on every integer
frame. The lid is rigidly attached to coupler C-D. Evaluation occurs at frame 63.

Concept load case for proportioning only: 35 kg lid, 0.45 m CG arm, two hinges sharing
load, approximately 77 N*m gravity moment per hinge before dynamic and slam factors.
This benchmark is a visual and kinematic concept, not a production or safety-certified design.
